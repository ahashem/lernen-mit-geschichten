# 07 Open questions

These are decisions that evidence could not settle from the desk. Each has a recommendation, so
that implementation is not blocked. Revisit each one once the listed test has been run.

| #   | Question                                                                                                    | Why it is open                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Recommendation                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | How to settle                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| --- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | **Gulzar or Noto Nastaliq Urdu** for Urdu reading?                                                          | Gulzar has tighter vertical metrics, and Noto is the more widely tested. Both are OFL and large. Rendering quality varies by browser and Android version.                                                                                                                                                                          | Gulzar first, with Noto Nastaliq Urdu as the fallback. Never fall back to Naskh.                                                                                                                                                                                 | Render 3 pages on Chrome Android, Safari iPad and a Chromebook. Ask 3 Urdu-reading parents which "looks like a real book".                                                                                                                                                          |
| 2   | **Digits in ar/ur:** Western (123), Arabic-Indic (١٢٣) or Extended (۱۲۳)?                                   | Families in Germany differ. School teaches Western digits, and heritage-language classes may teach the others.                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Western digits for page and chapter numbers and UI. Story text keeps whatever the translator wrote.                                                                                                                                                              | Ask 5 Arabic- and 5 Urdu-speaking parents.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| 3   | **Gendered address in Arabic** (and Urdu verb agreement) for "you" in child UI and feedback                 | Arabic second-person forms are gendered. We collect no gender.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Recorded narration and UI use forms the translators judge most neutral (e.g. imperatives rephrased as "let's…" / first-person plural). Avoid direct gendered "you" in child-facing strings.                                                                      | Translator workshop. Add a lint rule to the translation checklist.                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| 4   | **Recorded narration coverage.** Can volunteers record 5 languages × all stories?                           | Human narration is a principle, but it costs time. Device TTS for ar/ur/tr is uneven.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Publish a story in a locale only with recorded narration for bands 1-2. Bands 3-4 may publish text-first, with "Listen" hidden until a recording exists. Never use cloud TTS (it is a third-party request).                                                      | Track a backlog per locale. Revisit if any locale's backlog exceeds 10 stories.                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| 5   | **One host character** who welcomes and guides, or none?                                                    | A host helps pre-readers (the Khan Kids pattern) but risks parasocial pressure (Radesky) and reads as babyish to older readers.                                                                                                                                                                                                    | A host exists **only in Picture home** (the "?" tour, 404 spot) and never prompts unasked. Reader home has none.                                                                                                                                                 | Watch 3 children aged 4-5 with and without the tour.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| 6   | **Is "read before" marking already a mechanic?**                                                            | Informational and not rewarding, but some children may treat marks as collectables.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Keep it subtle (a 2 px spine mark, no count). Drop it if kita observations show children "collecting".                                                                                                                                                           | Observe in 2 kitas over 4 weeks.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| 7   | **Bands 3-4 free-text reflections stored locally**: privacy risk on shared devices?                         | Text could be personal, and the next pupil on a Chromebook could read it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Off by default. When on, show "This stays on this device. Others using it can see it. Delete it when done." with a one-tap delete on the end screen. Auto-delete after 7 days.                                                                                   | Ask 2 Klasse 5-6 teachers.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| 8   | **Print: browser print CSS or pre-built PDFs?**                                                             | Print CSS is zero-build, but output varies by browser (margins, SVG scaling). PDFs are consistent but heavier and need a build step.                                                                                                                                                                                               | Print CSS first. Pre-build PDFs only for the teacher "class packs" (photocopy masters), generated at build time.                                                                                                                                                 | Print the same pack from Chrome, Safari and Firefox on A4. Measure line weights.                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| 9   | **Two-screen Class room**: is `window.open` + BroadcastChannel reliable on school devices?                  | Pop-up blockers and managed Chromebooks can block it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Ship the single-screen drawer as the default, and two-screen as progressive enhancement with a clear failure message.                                                                                                                                            | Test on one managed Chromebook fleet.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| 10  | **Animal choice across communities**                                                                        | Some animals carry negative associations for some families (e.g. pigs, and dogs indoors, for some Muslim families). Others are culture-neutral.                                                                                                                                                                                    | Keep the recurring cast to broadly accepted animals (bear, fox, owl, rabbit, cat, hedgehog, turtle, bird). Existing characters are reviewed, not replaced, if they are already loved.                                                                            | Community reviewer pass on the cast.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| 11  | **How many feeling faces** for pre-readers: 6 or 8?                                                         | Fewer is easier to choose from, and more allows nuance (e.g. "jealous", "shy").                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | 6 on Picture home (happy, angry, sad, scared, calm, proud). The full set appears in the Library filter.                                                                                                                                                          | Card-sort with 6 children aged 4-6.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| 12  | **Which bilingual pairs** to support?                                                                       | German + any is the core need. ar+en or tr+en exist for some families.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Any pair where both translations exist. Order is chosen by the reader. There is no whitelist.                                                                                                                                                                    | n/a (a content-availability question, not a design one)                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| 13  | **Band boundaries per story**: who decides, by what rule?                                                   | Reading level differs by language (Turkish and German words are longer, Arabic has vocalisation).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Editors set the band per story using the length and density table in 01. The band is the same across locales, and a translator may flag "harder in my language".                                                                                                 | A moderator checklist. Review after 20 stories.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| 14  | **Should band 4 ever use photography or a different style** for non-fiction add-ons (e.g. internet safety)? | Older readers might find it more credible.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | No. Keep the riso style. Credibility comes from the content and writing, not the photos.                                                                                                                                                                         | Revisit only if non-fiction becomes a product line.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| 15  | **Default home on first visit**: Reader or Picture?                                                         | Most first visits are probably adults choosing. Pre-readers on a kita tablet need Picture home.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Reader home, with the prominent Picture-books door and the first-run "Who reads here?" card. Kita devices remember Picture home after one tap.                                                                                                                   | Test with 3 parents and 2 educators: time to start a band-1 story from a fresh device (< 20 s).                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| 16  | **`---` in existing story bodies**: rule between sections or page break?                                    | Existing Markdown uses `---` as a rule between `##` sections; the content model says `---` breaks are pages.                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | The reader treats `---` as page breaks only in a body with no `##` sections; otherwise it derives pages.                                                                                                                                                         | Owner decides the convention when editing Bruno.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| 17  | **Recordings versus derived pages**                                                                         | Bruno's two recordings (38 s, 51 s) do not match pages derived from the text, and a recording on the wrong page is worse than none.                                                                                                                                                                                                | Recordings are used only when pages are explicit; Bruno reads with the device voice until it has matching `---` breaks.                                                                                                                                          | Owner adds the breaks, or re-records per derived page.                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| 18  | **Cover art that is line art**                                                                              | The committed Bruno `cover.webp` is a portrait colouring page, not a 3:2 colour cover.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Shown contained on `--paper-2`; replace it when a colour cover exists.                                                                                                                                                                                           | Owner, with the art pipeline.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| 19  | **Device voices for Arabic and Urdu misread vocalised text**                                                | The site can only choose among a device's voices, not improve them.
