# Community and growth

Growth is trust spreading through kitas and families, not a funnel. Everything here respects `06`.

## Contributors

- **Moderators** (2–3): the only people who change content. Onboarding is a one-page checklist and a CMS invite.
- **Native reviewers**: volunteers who sign translations (`reviewedBy`) without touching the CMS beyond a review
  link or pull request. Credit them on the story page and in an About list.
- **Illustrators and narrators**, if the owner wants human art or voices later: the content model already carries
  `art`/`narrator` provenance, so human work slots in without a schema change.
- Open source: the repo is public; `CONTRIBUTING.md` explains code contributions.

## Feedback loop without tracking

- A low-friction "tell us about this story" link (a mailto or a form hosted by the owner, never a third-party
  widget on the page) on every story and on the About page.
- Kita partners: a short yearly conversation and a printed feedback card, because their questions shape the library
  more than clicks do.
- Content requests feed a public wishlist of skills not yet covered (the skills taxonomy shows the gaps).

## Partnerships

Kitas, libraries, speech-therapy and German-as-second-language networks, migrant-family organisations. Offer a
ready-made one-page handout (QR to the site, 3 recommended stories, the circle-time guide) per partner.

## Measuring

Decided (2026-10-24): Google Analytics through Google Tag Manager, only after consent on the banner (`AGENTS.md`
Analytics). A cookieless, aggregate-only, EU-hosted counter with no identifiers remains the alternative that would
need no banner. Track only: story opens, completion (reached the end), language, mode,
and offline installs, as daily aggregates.
