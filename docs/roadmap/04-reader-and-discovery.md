# Reader and discovery

Capabilities, not visuals: how it must look and move is in `ux/`. Each item has an acceptance line an agent can
verify in a browser.

## Reading

| Capability        | What it must do                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Accept when                                                                                                 |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Two reading modes | Scroll mode, and a page-by-page storybook for every story, standard or interactive                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | A standard story can be swiped through one passage at a time with pictures                                  |
| Pictures          | Cover and frames from `frames[]`; designed fallback from `emoji` + `palette` when art is absent                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | A story with no art and a story with full art both look finished                                            |
| Narration         | Plays `narration.audio` when present; otherwise browser read-aloud with word and sentence highlight                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Highlight stays in sync; a missing voice is reported, not silent                                            |
| Listen and follow | Play/pause, repeat page, speed, sleep timer that fades out                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Timer stops audio and leaves the page where it was                                                          |
| Resume            | Remembers position per story on the device                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Reopening offers "continue" at the page left                                                                |
| Quiet end         | A closing spread, then at most three talk cards (bands 3–4: think cards), one per screen; nothing is scored or counted                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Reflection answers are never marked wrong; the end offers talk, an activity or read again, never a dead end |
| Next              | Ends with a calm choice; at most one quiet tile for a story from the next shelf, marked by format only ("a chapter story, 3 chapters"). No autoplay of the next story                                                                                                                                                                    | Never suggests a story that does not exist in the reader's language                                         |
| Bilingual         | Two languages side by side or alternating; tap a sentence to hear either; remembers the pair                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | RTL/LTR pairs align correctly; works for any two available locales                                          |
| Who reads here    | Device setting "Who reads on this device?": Picture books / Read-along / Chapter stories / Long reads / Mixed. The **level belongs to the story (its `band`), not the child**; the setting only orders shelves and picks the icon-led or text-led home. Class links set it for pupil devices after a confirm card (`ux/01`, `ux/03` §15) | No child sees a level; no shelf is locked; nothing is applied silently                                      |
| Reading comfort   | Text size, line spacing, dyslexia-friendly face, bedtime theme                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Settings persist and apply to every reader screen                                                           |
| Colouring pages   | Open, print or save the line-art for the story                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Prints on A4 without cropping, black-and-white clean                                                        |

## Discovery

| Capability        | What it must do                                                                                                                                          | Accept when                                                                                 |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Find by feeling   | Home entry: "Today we want to talk about…" built on `feelings`/`situations`/skills                                                                       | Five or six everyday intents lead to a relevant shelf in ≤ 2 taps                           |
| Shelves           | Curated rows: new, series, seasonal/occasion, short for bedtime, with a colouring page                                                                   | Shelves populate from content; empty shelves do not render                                  |
| Shelves and grade | Browse by shelf (band) and Klasse hint (Kita, 1–6) for adults; show a story's format honestly                                                            | A Klasse 5 reader is never served only toddler stories, nor a 4-year-old only chapter books |
| Search and filter | Diacritic-insensitive across languages, skills/age/length filters, removable chips, URL state                                                            | Shared URLs reproduce the view; no-result state suggests alternatives                       |
| Availability      | Show which languages exist for each story; offer the reader's language first                                                                             | Language badges reflect reviewed translations only                                          |
| Bookmark and list | Bookmark per story (`{storyId, locale, chapter, offset}`) and an optional reading list the reader makes (bands 3–4). No counts, totals, dates or streaks | Works with no login; "forget this device" clears everything                                 |

## Content states the UI must handle

Draft hidden everywhere; story with no art; story with one language; narration missing or failing; audio blocked by
the browser; offline with only some stories cached; long titles in German; RTL title wrap.
