Once upon a time, there was a little turtle named Timo. Timo was not as fast as the rabbits, not as strong as the bears, and not as loud as the parrots.

One day, the frog called out in the forest: “Today we are having a big race! All animals can join!”

---

The animals cheered. But Timo sighed quietly and said: “I am slow… I will surely come last.”

---

Then his friend, the little mouse Mia, whispered: “Timo, you don’t have to be the fastest. Just be yourself. You can do more than you think!”

---

So the race began, and Timo’s heart was beating fast. The rabbits jumped quickly, the parrots flapped wildly, and the bears stomped loudly.

---

But halfway through, one rabbit stumbled, a parrot got stuck in a branch, and the bears grew tired. Timo, however, kept walking step by step, slowly but without stopping.

---

In the end, he reached the finish line – not first, but not last either. All the animals shouted: “Bravo, Timo! You did it because you believed in yourself!”

Timo smiled proudly and thought: “I am good just the way I am.”

---

From that day on, he no longer carried his shell as a burden, but as a shining shield full of confidence and self-respect.
