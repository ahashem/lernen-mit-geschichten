Once upon a time, there was a little raccoon named Fritzi. Fritzi had a big idea: he wanted to ride his small scooter down the tallest hill in the forest.

He shouted with joy: “This will be the best day ever!”

---

He stood on top of the hill, took a deep breath… and *whoooosh!* – off he went at full speed! But in the middle of the way, the scooter hit a stone, and *plump!* – Fritzi fell into the grass.

---

He said sadly: “Oh no! I am a total scooter failure!”

---

Suddenly, his friend, the owl Lotte, came flying. She said kindly, laughing: “Fritzi, do you know how many times I fell when I was learning to fly? Too many to count! But every time, I learned something new.”

---

Fritzi scratched his head and asked: “So… what did I learn?” Lotte smiled: “Maybe: Watch out for stones!”

---

Fritzi laughed loudly: “That’s true! The stone was tricky. But this time, I will be more careful!”

---

So he tried again. On the second attempt, he slipped too early – *splash!* – straight into a puddle. Now his fur was completely wet, and he looked very funny.

---

Instead of crying, Fritzi laughed: “Well, now I know that water and scooters are not friends.”

On the third attempt – no stones, no puddles, with lots of courage – Fritzi sped down the whole hill. “Yeees!” he shouted, and Lotte clapped her wings happily.

---

In the end, Fritzi said proudly: “You know, Lotte, mistakes teach us how to do better.”

And so, Fritzi learned that failure is not the end – but the beginning of a new try.
