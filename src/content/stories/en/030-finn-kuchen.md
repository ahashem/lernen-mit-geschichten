Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Finn. Finn loved cake. He loved chocolate cake, apple cake, strawberry cake – actually, he loved every kind of cake.

---

One day, Grandma baked a big, round chocolate cake. It smelled so delicious that Finn could hardly sit still. “Finn, the cake must cool down first,” Grandma said firmly. “Don’t touch it!”

---

Finn nodded obediently. But in his mind, he thought: “Just a tiny piece… no one will notice!”

---

So he sneaked into the kitchen, cut a little slice – and suddenly! The cake slipped, toppled off the plate, and plop – fell on the floor. Chocolate everywhere! On the carpet, on the dog Bello, and even on Finn’s nose.

---

Grandma came in and cried: “Oh dear! What happened here?”

Finn was about to say: “Uh… it was Bello!” But Bello was sitting innocently, just licking his paw.

---

Finn felt a tingle in his tummy. He remembered that his mother always said: “When you make a mistake, you must be honest. That’s what makes you brave!”

---

So he took a deep breath and said: “Grandma… it was my fault. I couldn’t wait and took a piece. I’m sorry.”

---

Grandma looked at him seriously – but then she smiled. “You know what, Finn? I’m proud of you for being honest. Mistakes happen. What matters is taking responsibility.”

---

Finn helped Grandma clean up the mess. And in the end, he got a fresh piece of cake – this time officially.

Since that day, whenever something went wrong, Finn would always say: “That was me – and I’ll fix it!”

---

And Bello? Of course, he got a little crumb of cake too. 🐶🍫
