In a small zoo lived a curious monkey named Milo. Milo could speak, but he had a special talent: He understood every gesture and every facial expression.

---

One sunny morning, Milo was playing in the garden. His friend, the turtle Lotte, came along. Lotte was very slow, and sometimes she was too shy to speak loudly.

---

“Hello, Lotte!” called Milo. But Lotte said nothing. She shrugged her shoulders and made a sad face.

---

Milo thought: 👉 Shrugging shoulders \= “I don’t know” or “I’m unsure.” 🙂 Sad face \= “Something is wrong.”

“Oh!” cried Milo. “You’re hungry, aren’t you?” Lotte nodded quickly and smiled.

---

Later, the parrot Koko came flying by. Koko flapped his wings and opened his eyes wide. Milo understood at once: 👉 Big eyes \= “Wow!” or “I’m surprised!” 👉 Flapping wings \= “I’m excited!”

---

Milo copied the gesture. Everyone laughed and flapped together.

At the end of the day, the animals played a new game: One makes a face or a movement – the others guess what it means. 🙈 😃 🤔 😴 😡
