Once upon a time, there was a little mouse named Lilo. Lilo lived in a big garden full of flowers, bees, and butterflies.

---

One day, Lilo and her friend, the hedgehog Fips, were playing ball. Suddenly, the ball fell right into a big puddle.

---

“This is your fault!” shouted Fips. “No, it was you!” shouted Lilo back.

---

They both looked angrily at each other. The mood was tense.

Then suddenly, Lilo giggled and said: “Look, Fips! The ball looks like a swimming tomato now!”

---

Fips couldn’t help but laugh. “Or like a wet strawberry with legs!”

They laughed so much that they forgot all their anger. Then they took the ball out of the puddle together.

---

Lilo said: “When we laugh, it’s much easier to solve problems.” Fips nodded: “Humor makes anger into little soap bubbles that pop right away.”

And so, they played again – happy, wet, and giggling.
