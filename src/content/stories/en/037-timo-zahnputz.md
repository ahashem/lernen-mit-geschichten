Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Timo. Timo was cheerful, curious – and a little forgetful.

---

In the morning, he often jumped out of bed, put on his socks, and ran straight to the kitchen. “Breakfast!” he shouted. But … most of the time, he forgot to brush his teeth.

---

One day, his mother gave him a little alarm clock in the shape of a squirrel. “Ring ring ring!” went the clock. And with a funny voice, it said: “Timooo! Have you brushed your teeth yet?”

---

Timo laughed out loud. “A talking alarm clock! How funny!”

From that day on, the squirrel-alarm clock helped Timo learn his daily routine:

---

Morning: brush teeth, wash face, make bed.

Afternoon: do homework first, then play.

Evening: put toys away, put on pajamas, brush teeth (again!), and go to bed.

---

Sometimes, Timo tried to trick it. He said: “I already brushed my teeth!” But the clock sniffed his toothbrush. “Hmm … dry! Back to the bathroom, my friend!”

---

Timo giggled and ran back to brush his teeth.

After a few weeks, Timo hardly needed the clock anymore. He remembered by himself: “First teeth, then play!” “First homework, then football!”

---

The squirrel-alarm clock nodded happily. “Bravo, Timo! Now you are a real routine master.”

And so, Timo learned that small habits make us stronger every day – and that even a funny alarm clock can sometimes be the best friend.

---

The End.
