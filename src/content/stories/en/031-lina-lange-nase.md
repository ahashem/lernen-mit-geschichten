Once upon a time, there was a little mouse named Lina. Lina was quick and clever, but sometimes she was also a bit impatient.

---

One morning, she was playing “hide and seek” with her friends in the forest. Everything was going well until her cheeky friend, the frog Pipo, revealed her hiding place. Everyone laughed, and Lina felt her nose grow hot with anger.

---

She wanted to shout loudly: “That’s not fair!” Her tiny paws already curled into little fists.

But then she remembered something her grandmother always said: “If you feel like you’re about to explode with anger, lift your nose up high and count to three!”

---

So Lina lifted her little nose, took a deep breath, and counted: One… two… three.

---

Suddenly, she burst out laughing. Her nose was so high in the air that it almost bumped into a branch! Her friends laughed too – even Pipo.

---

Instead of scolding, Lina said: “Okay, this time you were faster. But in the next game, it’s my turn!”

The anger disappeared, and the game went on with joy and laughter. At the end of the day, Lina felt proud: She had tamed her anger – and kept her friends.
