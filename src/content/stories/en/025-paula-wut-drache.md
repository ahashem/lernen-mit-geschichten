Once upon a time there was a girl named Paula. Paula was cheerful and curious – but sometimes she became angry very quickly.

---

Whenever someone took her toy or she lost in a game, she felt as if a little dragon woke up inside her tummy. The dragon’s name was Funkel.

---

“GRRRR!” roared Funkel whenever Paula got angry. “Shout! Stomp! Throw your teddy!” he demanded.

---

But Paula noticed something: when she did that, her friends ran away. Nobody wanted to play when Funkel was raging.

🐉 The Plan

---

One day Paula said: “Funkel, I don’t want you to ruin everything. We need a plan.”

The little dragon was surprised. “A plan? For anger?”

---

Paula nodded. “Yes! We’ll practice tricks to make you smaller.”

The Three Tricks

---

1. Breathing like a dragon: Paula blew out air slowly, as if she were blowing out candles. Each time she did it, Funkel became smaller.

---

2. Counting like a wizard: Paula counted slowly: “One… two… three… four… five…” until Funkel grew calmer.

---

3. Funny faces: Paula looked in the mirror, stuck out her tongue, and made silly faces. Funkel laughed so hard that he almost forgot to be angry.

---

🎉 The Ending

The next day Paula lost in a game again. Funkel wanted to roar – but Paula whispered: “Breathe… count… make a funny face!”

---

She blew, she counted, she made a silly face. And suddenly Funkel laughed and shrank into a tiny dragon, who only wanted to giggle.

Her friends clapped: “Paula, you’re the best anger-magician!”

---

Paula smiled. She now knew: Anger is normal – but you can learn to be the boss of your feelings.

---

And Funkel? He stayed – but only to laugh, never to rage.
