Story: Lina and the Missing Bridge

---

Once upon a time, in a small village at the edge of a big forest, there lived a cheerful girl named Lina. Lina was known for her curiosity and her crazy ideas.

---

One day, she wanted to visit her friends on the other side of the river. But – oh no! – the old wooden bridge had disappeared, and only a few planks were floating in the water.

---

“Oh dear! How will I get across?” cried Lina.

The villagers stood there helpless. One of them said: “Let’s just wait until someone builds a new bridge.” Another said: “Then we’ll just stay at home.”

---

But Lina started to think 🧐: “There must be another way!”

---

She looked around and saw big pumpkins from the village farm lying by the riverbank. Suddenly she smiled and said: “Aha! I have an idea!”

---

She took the pumpkins, tied them together with ropes, and built a floating raft. The people were astonished. Some laughed and said: “That will never work!”

---

But Lina bravely sat on the pumpkin raft. It rocked a little, but it held. Slowly, she paddled with a piece of wood until she reached the other side safely.

---

The children on the opposite bank cheered: “Lina, you are brilliant! You made a bridge out of pumpkins!”

From that day on, the villagers called Lina the Idea Finder. And whenever they faced a problem, they asked: “What would Lina do?”
