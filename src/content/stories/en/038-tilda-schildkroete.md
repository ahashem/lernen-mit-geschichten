Once upon a time there was a little turtle named Tilda. Tilda loved swimming in the pond. But she often thought to herself: “My legs are so short. I can’t swim as fast as the fish!”

---

One day, the frog Fredi called from the shore: “Tilda, come on! Let’s have a swimming race!” Tilda blushed and said: “But I am much slower than you…”

The duck Dora quacked kindly: “Being slow is not bad. Everyone has their own pace.”

---

Tilda sighed, but she tried anyway. She started swimming slowly – splish, splash – and of course, the fish had already reached the finish line. But on her way, Tilda saw colorful stones at the bottom, a sleeping crab, and even a silver shell.

---

When she finally arrived at the finish line, all the animals shouted: “Wow, Tilda! You discovered the most beautiful things!”

Tilda laughed and said: “Maybe I am not the fastest, but I have good eyes and lots of patience.”

---

From that day on, she stopped comparing herself to others. She kept swimming, practiced hard, became stronger – but always at her own pace.

And if the animals have a swimming race today, Frog Fredi laughs and says: “Let Tilda go first, she will probably find another treasure!”
