At a small school, there lived a cheerful squirrel named Timo. Timo loved the big break, because he could play with all the animals.

---

One day, Timo ran onto the schoolyard. There, the rabbit Lila, the fox Fredi, and the owl Emma were already playing.

---

Timo shouted loudly: “I want to slide right now!” Then he pushed himself to the front.


Lila said: “Wait, Timo! Here, everyone stands in line and waits for their turn.” Timo smiled and said: “But I’m fast! I’ll slide only for a few seconds!”

---

The friends were not happy. Fredi crossed his arms, and Emma shook her head in disapproval. Then Timo noticed that the mood was changing.

---

Suddenly, the teacher Mr. Bear called: “Timo, do you know what is important during the break?” Timo thought… “To have fun?” Mr. Bear nodded: “Yes, but also consideration. That means: waiting, sharing, and listening.”

---

Timo sighed and stood at the back of the line. At first, he felt bored, but soon he found it fun: He talked with Emma, shared jokes with Fredi, and laughed with Lila while they were waiting.

When it was finally his turn, he slid down and shouted: “Waiting makes the game even more fun!”

---

Everyone laughed. In the end, Timo said: “I’m sorry for pushing in front of you.” The friends shouted: “It’s okay, Timo! You learned quickly!”
