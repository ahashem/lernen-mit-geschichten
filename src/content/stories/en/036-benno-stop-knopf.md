In a small village, there lived a cheerful boy named Benno. Benno was kind and funny – but he had a little problem: Whenever something happened, he reacted immediately. Sometimes he shouted, sometimes he stamped his feet, and sometimes he laughed far too loudly.

---

One day, Benno was playing ball with his friend Lilli. Suddenly, the ball fell into the water. Benno almost shouted: “Oh no, that’s your fault!” But then he heard a quiet voice:

“Benno, press the Stop Button!”

---

Benno looked around – and indeed: On his chest blinked a little, invisible Magic Stop Button. Only he could feel it.

---

Benno pressed the button. Click! Everything became quiet in his head for a moment. He took a breath in, breathed out … and thought: “Wait a minute. It’s just a ball. We can fish it out with a stick.”

---

Lilli laughed: “Good idea!” And together they got the ball out of the water.

From that day on, Benno used his Magic Stop Button whenever he got angry or impatient:

When his little brother took his toy, he pressed: Click! – and said: “Let’s share.”

---

When the teacher gave him a difficult task, he pressed: Click! – and thought: “I’ll try step by step.”

When the dog barked and startled him, he pressed: Click! – and giggled: “Oh, he just wants to play.”

---

In the end, Benno realized: The Magic Stop Button wasn’t a real button. It was in his heart – and everyone could have one.

From then on, Benno wasn’t only cheerful and curious, but also wise in how he reacted. And all the children in the village wanted to know: “Where can we get such a Magic Stop Button?”

---

Benno winked and said: “Try it yourself. Each of you already has one!”
