Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lina. Lina was cheerful, curious – and sometimes a little impatient.

---

One day she was practicing riding her bike without training wheels. She fell down. Thud! “Oh no!” said Lina. “I must not be good enough.”

---

Suddenly, she heard a quiet voice. It was her mirror, which she had taken out into the garden to play with. The mirror giggled and sang: “You can do it, if you tell yourself: I am strong, I’ll try again!”

---

Lina laughed. “A singing mirror? That’s crazy!” But she tried saying it: “I am strong! I’ll try again!”

---

She got back on her bike. Cling! – this time she rode a bit further. “Hooray!” shouted Lina.

---

She clapped her hands and said to herself: “Well done, Lina! Just a little more!”

And every time she almost fell, she sang her little song: “I am strong, I’ll try again!”

At the end of the day, she could ride her bike – without training wheels.

---

Mom came into the garden and asked: “Lina, who helped you?” Lina smiled and said: “Myself! My mirror just made a bit of music.”

And so Lina learned: The best reward is when you encourage yourself and feel proud of who you are.
