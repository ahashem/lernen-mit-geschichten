Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Lukas.

Lukas loved chocolate more than anything else.

---

One afternoon, he was playing in the garden with his best friend Mia. Mia had a little colorful car that she really liked.

Suddenly, Lukas thought: “If I let Mia’s car roll down the ramp, it will look super cool!”

---

He grabbed the car – without asking – and sent it down the ramp. But then… CRASH! – the car hit the fence, and one of its wheels broke off.

---

Mia looked at him with big eyes and said: “Lukas! That was my favorite car!”

---

Lukas’s face turned red. His tummy felt heavy, like a big stone of chocolate. He wanted to run away.

---

But then he remembered something his grandma always said: “If you make a mistake, be honest. An honest ‘I’m sorry’ makes hearts feel light again.”

---

Lukas took a deep breath, went to Mia, and said: “Mia… I’m sorry. I took your car without asking. It was my fault. Please forgive me. I didn’t want it to break.”

---

Mia looked at him seriously for a moment. Then she smiled and said: “Thank you, Lukas. Because you were honest, I can forgive you. But you have to help me fix it, okay?”

---

Lukas nodded quickly – and they both laughed. Together they repaired the colorful little car. In the end, it rolled again, almost like new.
