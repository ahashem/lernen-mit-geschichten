Max is a little boy. Today, he is at the park. In the park, there is a big climbing wall. Max wants to climb it, but the wall is very, very tall!

---

Max climbs and climbs. Suddenly—oh no! Max is stuck! He can't get down.

---

Max thinks, “What should I do now?” Then he shouts loudly, “Help! Help!”

His friend Anna hears him. She runs over and says, “Max, don’t worry! I’ll help you!” Anna brings a big tree log and puts it under Max.

---

Max carefully climbs down onto the log and then to the ground. At the bottom, Max says, “Thank you, Anna! You helped me!”

Anna laughs, “Sometimes we all need help, and that’s okay!”

---

Max learns: If you need help, just ask! Everything is more fun that way!
