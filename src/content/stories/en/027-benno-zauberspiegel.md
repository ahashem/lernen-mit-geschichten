Once upon a time, there was a cheerful boy named Benno. Benno loved to play, laugh, and make jokes all day long, but sometimes he didn’t realize that his behavior could bother others.

---

One day, Benno found a magic mirror in the forest. To his surprise, the mirror could speak!

---

The mirror said in a deep voice: “Hello, Benno! I have a task for you: Observe your behavior! Look carefully — is it kind and helpful, or sometimes maybe too wild?”

---

Benno asked curiously: “And how do I do that?”

---

The mirror smiled and said: “It’s very easy! Ask yourself three questions:

---

1. Was I kind? 2. Did I help someone or annoy someone? 3. Am I satisfied with my behavior?”

---

The next day, Benno tried it out.

---

At breakfast, he spilled some milk and laughed: “Oops!” Then he asked himself: “Was I kind?” – Yes, nobody was angry. “Did I annoy anyone?” – No, it was just a small accident. “Am I satisfied?” – Hmm… I should have been a bit more careful.

---

At school, he helped his friend Mia pick up her pencils. “Was I kind?” – Yes! “Did I help someone?” – Yes! “Am I satisfied?” – Oh yes, very much!

---

In the evening, Benno looked into the magic mirror again. The mirror nodded and said: “Well done, Benno! You are a good observer of yourself. Whoever watches his own actions can become a little better every day.”

---

Benno smiled proudly and said: “Then I am my own superhero!” And from that day on, he proudly called himself: Benno, the Behavior Detective 🕵️‍♂️✨
